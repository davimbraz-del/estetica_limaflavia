# Surface: Landing page (index.html)

**Mode:** Persuade. **Build path:** code-first (user's answer).

**Audience / job:** women 25–60, Recife, from Instagram on a phone, warm. Job: feel cared for and safe, then open WhatsApp with Flávia.
**Action:** WhatsApp click (hero composer, per-service "Quero saber mais", objections CTA, final CTA, floating button).
**Proof / content:** copy.md v1.0. Only real proof: congress participation. Testimonials, photos and before/after are marked placeholders.
**Constraints:** pinned palette and feel (PRODUCT.md Brand Commitments); no red, countdown, pop-up; WCAG AA; mobile-first; single static file.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A spa's care card, not a clinic brochure. The page refuses the category default (stock spa photo hero, row of equal service cards, "agende já" banner) and instead opens with a working note to Flávia: the visitor says what hurts and where she wants to be cared for, and the message is already written.

OWN-WORLD: White and #FBF8FD ground; pastel lilac #E9DDF7/#D9C6F0 fields that own whole sections, separated by soft hand-drawn waves; lavender #B79CE0 fine-line leaves and sprigs (1.25px stroke, round caps); deep lilac #6E4BA8-range only on buttons and short titles; rose-tea #F6E4EC as a quiet second field. Rounded 28px+ corners, pill controls, diffuse lilac-tinted shadows with offset. Serif display (Gilda Display), script only for "Flávia Lima" (Pinyon Script), rounded body (Nunito, 18px).

STORY: She recognises her own tiredness and pain, learns one person treats skin and body gently, at the studio or at home, sees the honest menu of care, has each fear answered softly, and sends a message that already says what she needs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: script "Flávia Lima" small over the serif headline "Trate sua dor, sem dor." at ~2.6rem, subheadline, then the care note: a rounded paper card with chips ("dor nas costas", "pele", "inchaço", "relaxar") and a two-way toggle "no espaço / na minha casa", primary pill "Enviar para a Flávia" inside the card, micro-copy under it. Desktop: headline column left, the note card right, overlapping an arch-framed portrait placeholder with a sprig drawn across it.

FORM: Spa care card / treatment menu (the spa's printed "cardápio de cuidados" with dotted leaders, carried into the services list). seed: none (pinned) — user brief: "Sensação: um spa calmo e uma mão que acolhe. Formas orgânicas, cantos arredondados, ondas suaves entre as seções, sombras difusas, ilustrações em traço fino de folhas e flores, muito respiro." and "Cores da marca: branco + lilás/roxo, SEMPRE em tons PASTEL" (marked OBRIGATÓRIA, "o briefing vence"). Signature interaction: the care-note composer that writes the WhatsApp message live. Motion grammar: slow fade-and-rise reveals with exponential ease-out, a sprig that draws its stroke once in the hero; all off under prefers-reduced-motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
