# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static: a single `index.html` (HTML, CSS, minimal vanilla JS), no framework, no build step. Images live in `img/`. Must deploy as-is to Netlify, Hostinger or GitHub Pages.

## Users

Women aged 25 to 60 in Recife/PE (DDD 81), arriving mostly from Instagram (@estetica_limaflavia) on a phone, warm audience (they already follow or know Flávia). They carry body tension and pain, skin concerns (acne, spots, dryness, signs of age), little time for self-care, and distrust of impersonal clinics. Their job on the site: understand what Flávia does, feel safe, and start a WhatsApp conversation.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Flávia Lima, esthetician and massage therapist at Viver Bem – Estética Facial e Corporal (@espacoviverbemoficial). It turns Instagram visits into WhatsApp conversations (primary: +55 81 99495-1191; secondary: +55 81 99594-7631). Success = clicks to WhatsApp, ideally with a pre-filled message naming the service.

## Positioning

One professional who joins facial esthetics and massage therapy, treating skin and body with the same hands, under the motto "Massoterapeuta, trate sua dor, sem dor". Scheduled appointments only (hora marcada), at the studio or at the client's home (atendimento em domicílio).

## Operating Context

Visitors read on mobile, often in short sessions from an Instagram bio link or a Meta ad. Conversion happens off-site in WhatsApp, where Flávia does an initial needs assessment and quotes prices. Services: facial (limpeza de pele, peeling, pele acneica, pele de seda, rejuvenescedor, pele de vidro, jato de plasma, laser/LED terapia, revitalização labial [technique name to confirm]); body (massoterapia, massagem relaxante, massagem para dores, drenagem linfática); integrative (harmonização energética com cristais e chakras).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No prices published: "valores sob consulta no WhatsApp".
- Undecided / pending with Flávia: prices, home-visit regions, labial technique name, payment methods, studio address, real photos, real testimonials.
- No real scarcity: no countdowns, no "últimas vagas", no pop-ups.
- No aesthetic medical claims (no "cura", "elimina"); before/after always carries "Resultados podem variar".

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: close, gentle, feminine, like a friend who cares; no pressure, no guru jargon; 💜 used sparingly in copy.
- Palette pinned by the user: white #FFFFFF / off-white #FBF8FD, pastel lilac #E9DDF7 / #D9C6F0, lavender #B79CE0, accent purple #8E6CC4 (darkened where AA requires), optional rose-tea #F6E4EC, text #3D3550 (never pure black). Always pastel, conveying acolhimento.
- Feel pinned by the user: a calm spa and a welcoming hand; organic forms, rounded corners, soft waves between sections, diffuse shadows, fine-line leaf and flower drawings, generous whitespace.
- Type roles pinned: elegant serif headings, delicate script only for the name "Flávia Lima", rounded legible sans for body at 16–18px.
- Forbidden: red, countdowns, pop-ups, aggressive visuals.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: participation in esthetics congresses (e.g. Imersão Estética Erro Zero, as congressista); white lab coat, professional and welcoming appearance; Instagram post voice ("Massagem não é luxo. É saúde, é amor-próprio, é autocuidado.").
- Absent, must not be fabricated: testimonials, client counts, years of experience, ratings, before/after photos, photos of Flávia. Use clearly marked placeholders.

## Product Principles

1. Acolhimento first: every element should lower anxiety before asking for action.
2. Honest by default: no invented proof, scarcity, or results.
3. One tap to Flávia: WhatsApp is always reachable, and messages arrive already saying what the client wants.
4. Readable for 60+: large body type, high contrast, generous touch targets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text 17–18px, touch targets ≥48px, respects prefers-reduced-motion, works without JS for reading and for reaching WhatsApp.
